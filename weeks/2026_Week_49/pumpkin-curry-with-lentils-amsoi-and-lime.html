Pumpkin curry with lentils, amsoi and lime

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Use the big pot for cooking the rice. The frying pan will serve as your "wok or sauté pan" for building the curry base and simmering the stew. The medium pot, oven, and air fryer are not required for this recipe (steps 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7).
  • Start the rice in the big pot and then immediately begin the curry preparation in the frying pan to efficiently cook both components in parallel (steps 1, 2).
  • Before turning on any heat, fully chop the onion, celery, garlic, and ginger; slice, peel, deseed, and cube the butternut squash; rinse and prep the amsoi; and grate/squeeze the lime. Have all measured spices, coconut milk, water, and stock cube ready for quick additions (steps 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8).
  • Be ready to add the butternut squash immediately after the garlic, ginger, and spices have cooked for a minute, as these aromatics can burn quickly. Crucially, add the amsoi leaves only at the very end of cooking and stir just until they are wilted to prevent overcooking (steps 4, 7).
  • When mashing some butternut squash against the side of the pan, use the back of a spoon to create a naturally thicker and creamier sauce without completely pureeing all the pumpkin pieces (step 6).
  • Taste the curry before adding the final lime juice to adjust seasoning, then stir in the zest and some juice to brighten the flavors, allowing for additional fresh lime to be added at the table (steps 8, 9).
