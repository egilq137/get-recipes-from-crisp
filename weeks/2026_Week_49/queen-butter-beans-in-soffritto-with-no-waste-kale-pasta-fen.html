Queen Butter beans in soffritto with No Waste kale pasta, fennel salad and homemade cashew parmesan

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Use the frying pan for the soffritto base, and the big pot for boiling the pasta. The medium pot, oven, and air fryer are not needed for this recipe (steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10).
  • Before heating, chop all vegetables for the soffritto (onion, celery, carrot, chilli, garlic, rosemary) and prepare the fennel for the salad. This will allow you to add ingredients smoothly as the soffritto cooks (steps 1, 2, 4, 7).
  • Start the soffritto in the frying pan and get the big pot of salted water boiling for pasta early. While the soffritto simmers, make the cashew 'Parmesan' using your hand blender and prepare the fennel salad (steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9).
  • When adding the peeled tomatoes to the soffritto, mash them finely with a fork or wooden spoon directly in the pan to release their flavour and integrate them better into the sauce (step 5).
  • Be careful not to over-grind the cashew 'Parmesan' in your hand blender; pulse it just until it forms a grainy powder to avoid turning it into a paste (step 9).
  • Taste the butter bean soffritto after adding the beans and liquid, then adjust the salt and add a generous amount of black pepper before stirring in the fresh parsley for the best flavour (step 10).
