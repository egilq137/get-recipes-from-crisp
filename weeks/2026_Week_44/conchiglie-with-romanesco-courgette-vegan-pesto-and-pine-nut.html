Chef's notes & cookware

  • Your big pot is for the pasta. Use your medium pot for the Romanesco florets, replacing the 'small pan' in the recipe. The frying pan is needed for the courgette later (steps 1, 2, 5, 6, 7).
  • To save time and free up your frying pan, toast the pine nuts in the air fryer or oven at about 180°C/350°F for 3-5 minutes, keeping a close eye on them, while the pasta and Romanesco cook. This lets you move straight to frying the courgette without cleaning the pan (steps 3, 6).
  • Before any heat goes on, get all your chopping done: Romanesco into florets, spinach rinsed and chopped, courgette into quarter moons, and chilli and garlic prepped. This ensures a smooth workflow, especially when things start cooking quickly (steps 2, 4, 6).
  • Start the pasta first, then the Romanesco about 3-4 minutes later, as it cooks faster. Be ready to add the chopped spinach to the pasta for just the last 30 seconds to wilt it perfectly, then drain everything quickly (steps 1, 2, 5).
  • Ensure both pasta and Romanesco are cooked al dente for the best texture. When mixing, use a little of the pasta cooking liquid if needed to create a light sauce with the pesto, and don't skimp on seasoning at the end (steps 1, 2, 5, 7).
