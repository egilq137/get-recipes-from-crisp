Fried rice with leek, pak choi and a fried egg in miso butter

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Your frying pan will handle all stir-frying for the carrots, leek, pak choi stems, and the final rice mix. Use the medium pot for cooking rice and then for preparing your miso butter. The air fryer or oven is perfect for crisping the tofu (steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9).
  • Get ahead by prepping all your vegetables and tofu before any heat goes on: peel and cut carrots, clean and slice leek, separate and chop pak choi, slice spring onions, and pat dry and cube your tofu (steps 2, 3, 4, 5, 6).
  • While your rice simmers in the medium pot and you're stir-frying the vegetables in the frying pan, utilize the air fryer or oven to crisp up your tofu blocks. This keeps the single frying pan free for the main stir-fry (steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 6).
  • Start your rice immediately in the medium pot, as it requires 20 minutes to cook. Once it's simmering, you can efficiently move on to preparing and cooking your vegetables and tofu in parallel (steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 6).
  • For truly crispy tofu, ensure you leave it undisturbed for the full 5 minutes on each side in the air fryer or oven. When incorporating the pak choi leaves into the fried rice, stir-fry them very briefly to retain their fresh crunch (steps 6, 9).
