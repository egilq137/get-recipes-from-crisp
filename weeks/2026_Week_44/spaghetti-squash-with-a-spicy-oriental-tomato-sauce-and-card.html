Spaghetti squash with a spicy Oriental tomato sauce and cardamom rice

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Your cookware plan: Use the oven for the spaghetti squash from preheating through baking. The medium pot is for cooking the cardamom rice. The frying pan will be used for sautéing the onion, frying the mince and apple, and simmering the spicy tomato sauce. Use the air fryer for frying the spring onions in step 7, as your frying pan will be occupied with the sauce (steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9).
  • Maximize parallel cooking by starting the spaghetti squash in the oven and the rice in the medium pot simultaneously. Once those are going, begin preparing your sauce in the frying pan to ensure everything finishes around the same time (steps 1, 2, 3, 4).
  • Before you turn on any heat, complete your mise en place: chop the onion, grate the apple, crush the cardamom pods, press the garlic, chop the coriander (reserving some), and slice the orange. Also, prep the squash by halving, seeding, oiling, and pricking it (steps 2, 3, 4, 5, 6).
  • The sauce simmers for 10 minutes while the squash finishes in the oven; ensure you have the spring onions cut and ready to go into the air fryer as soon as the rice is done, so they can be mixed in fresh (steps 6, 7, 8).
  • When checking the spaghetti squash, a fork should easily pull away the strands; aim for tender but still with a slight bite. Remember to season the sauce to taste before serving, and encourage guests to pull apart the squash strands themselves (steps 8, 9).
