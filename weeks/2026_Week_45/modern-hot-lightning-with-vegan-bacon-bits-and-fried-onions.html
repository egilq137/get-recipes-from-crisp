Chef's notes & cookware

  • Use the big pot for boiling the potatoes. The single frying pan is for frying the apple and onion. The air fryer is perfect for crisping the vegan bacon bits, freeing up the frying pan (steps 1, 2, 4).
  • Before turning on any heat, prepare all vegetables: peel and cut potatoes, apples, and onions, then slice chicory and chop dill. This ensures a smooth workflow once cooking begins (steps 1, 2, 3).
  • Start the potatoes in the big pot first as they take the longest. While they cook, begin frying the apple and onion in the frying pan, and simultaneously crisp the vegan bacon bits in the air fryer. This maximizes efficiency (steps 1, 2, 4).
  • Don't forget to reserve some potato cooking liquid before draining; it's key for a creamy mash without adding too much fat. Mash thoroughly and season generously to taste (step 5).
  • For an added layer of texture and freshness, hold back some raw chicory to use as a garnish. A pinch of cinnamon with the apples enhances their sweetness and warmth (steps 2, 5, 6).
