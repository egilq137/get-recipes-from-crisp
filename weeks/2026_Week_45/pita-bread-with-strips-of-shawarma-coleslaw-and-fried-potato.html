Pita bread with strips of shawarma, coleslaw and fried potatoes

Chef's notes & cookware

  • The oven is essential for roasting the potatoes and warming the pita bread. Use the frying pan for browning the shawarma strips; the big and medium pots are not needed for this recipe (steps 2, 4, 7).
  • Maximize efficiency by starting the shawarma in the frying pan while the potatoes are baking in the oven. For the pita bread, use the air fryer to warm them quickly and free up the oven if needed (steps 2, 4, 7).
  • Before any heat goes on, prepare all vegetables: peel and cube potatoes, thinly slice cabbage and grate carrots, and peel and grate beetroot and chop coriander. This makes assembly and cooking much smoother (steps 2, 3, 6).
  • Start the potatoes immediately after preheating the oven as they have the longest cooking time. Plan to warm the pita bread only in the final minutes before serving to ensure they are fresh and warm (steps 1, 2, 7).
  • For the coleslaw, thoroughly knead the cabbage and carrot with salt to soften them properly before adding other ingredients, which greatly improves the final texture. Ensure the potatoes are spread in a single layer on the baking tray for optimal crispiness (steps 2, 3).
