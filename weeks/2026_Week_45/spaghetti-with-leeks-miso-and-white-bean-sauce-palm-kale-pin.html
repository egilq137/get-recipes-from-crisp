Spaghetti with leeks, miso and white bean sauce, palm kale, pine nuts and a chicory and apple salad

Week 45, 2026 · 30 min active · ~58 min realistic (incl. prep) · 4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation

Amounts in the steps are for 2 servings, as on crisp.nl.

Tap a step when it's done.

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Use the big pot for boiling spaghetti and kale, and the frying pan for simmering leeks and combining the sauce. Toast the pine nuts in the air fryer since the frying pan will be busy (steps 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8).
  • Before any heat goes on, prepare all your vegetables: strip and cut the palm kale, slice the leeks, and dice the apple and chicory for the salad. This ensures a smooth workflow (steps 2, 4, 9).
  • As soon as the spaghetti hits the big pot, start the leeks simmering gently in the frying pan; this allows both main components to cook simultaneously. While the pasta and leeks are cooking, use the air fryer to toast the pine nuts for garnish (steps 3, 4, 8).
  • Blend your bean and miso sauce while the leeks are softening; this ensures the sauce is ready to pour over them as soon as they are tender, preventing any waiting time (steps 4, 5, 6).
  • When blending the sauce, add the pasta cooking water gradually to achieve a perfectly smooth and pourable consistency; remember to reserve extra pasta water when draining to adjust the final dish (steps 5, 7).
