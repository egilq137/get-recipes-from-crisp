Mini köfte burger with fried potatoes, leek, fennel and dill yoghurt sauce

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Your cookware plan: Use the oven for roasting the potatoes. Repurpose your air fryer for the fennel and leek to achieve tender, slightly browned vegetables. The frying pan will then be free to handle the köfte burgers (steps 2, 3, 4).
  • Before any heat goes on, get your mise en place ready: wash and cube the potatoes, then prep and slice the fennel and leek. This front-loads the work and ensures a smooth cooking flow (steps 2, 3).
  • Start the potatoes roasting in the oven first as they require the longest cooking time. Immediately after, get your prepped fennel and leek into the air fryer. Once the vegetables are nearly done, begin frying the köfte burgers in the frying pan so everything finishes around the same time (steps 2, 3, 4).
  • While the main components are cooking, quickly assemble your dill yoghurt sauce. This can be done in a spare moment and will be ready to serve as soon as the hot elements are plated (step 5).
  • For the vegetables, aim for a good caramelization and slight browning, not just tenderness, as this develops a richer flavor. Similarly, ensure the köfte burgers achieve a nice, golden-brown crust for texture and taste (steps 3, 4).
