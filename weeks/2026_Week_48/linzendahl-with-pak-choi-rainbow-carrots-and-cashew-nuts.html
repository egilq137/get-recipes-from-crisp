Linzendahl with pak choi, rainbow carrots and cashew nuts

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Your frying pan will be used for the dahl (Step 2, replacing the wok), the medium pot for the basmati rice, and the oven for roasting the rainbow root. The big pot and air fryer remain available if you need them (steps 2, 3, 4).
  • Maximize efficiency by starting the dahl in the frying pan and the rainbow root in the oven concurrently, as they both require similar cook times. After about 10 minutes, begin cooking the rice in the medium pot so everything finishes around the same time (steps 2, 3, 4).
  • Get ahead by finely chopping the yellow onion and garlic for the dahl, and peeling and cutting the rainbow root before you start cooking. Also, cut the pak choi stems and leaves, keeping them separate as they are added at different times (steps 2, 3, 5).
  • Be mindful when adding the pak choi leaves; they only need a minute or two to wilt in the hot dahl, so add them just before serving to maintain their texture and bright green color (step 5).
  • Elevate the dish by lightly toasting the cashew nuts in the air fryer for 3-5 minutes at 180°C (or a dry pan) until golden brown and fragrant, which significantly boosts their flavour and crunch (step 6).
