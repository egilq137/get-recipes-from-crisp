Creamy pasta with Brussels sprouts, sweet potato, portobello mushrooms and sunflower seed crunch

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Plan your cookware: use the big pot for pasta, the frying pan for the sweet potatoes, sprouts, and later the Portobello mushrooms, and the medium pot for the sunflower seeds (steps 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7).
  • Before turning on any heat, complete all peeling, slicing, chopping, and zesting for the sweet potatoes, sprouts, garlic, Portobello, and lemon (steps 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9).
  • Start the pasta water in the big pot early, then begin the vegetables in the frying pan; simultaneously, use the medium pot for the sunflower seeds to save time (steps 1, 3, 4, 5).
  • Pay close attention to the sunflower seeds, adding the garlic and lemon zest only at the very end to prevent burning, then remove from heat immediately (step 5).
  • Always save some pasta cooking water before draining, as it's crucial for achieving the perfect creamy sauce consistency when mixing everything together (steps 4, 8).
  • Finish the dish by mixing in a fresh squeeze of lemon juice and garnishing with finely chopped parsley just before serving to elevate the flavors (steps 8, 9).
