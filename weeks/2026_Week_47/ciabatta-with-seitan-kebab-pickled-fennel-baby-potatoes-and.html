Ciabatta with seitan kebab, pickled fennel, baby potatoes and a red chicory and spinach salad

Week 47, 2026 · 30 min active · ~62 min realistic (incl. prep) · 4 servings

Preparation

Amounts in the steps are for 2 servings, as on crisp.nl.

Tap a step when it's done.

Chef's notes & cookware

  • Your oven is for the main batch of potatoes and the ciabatta. Cook the kohlrabi wedges in the air fryer for a crispy finish, seasoned with the potatoes but in their own space. Reserve the frying pan for the quick-cooking seitan kebab (steps 1, 3, 6).
  • Before any heat, tackle all your chopping: peel and slice kohlrabi, wash and wedge potatoes, thinly slice fennel, and cut chicory. This prep allows for seamless cooking once you start (steps 2, 4, 5).
  • Get the potatoes into the oven and the kohlrabi into the air fryer right away. While they roast, use the free time to prepare your pickled fennel, whisk the vinaigrette, and mix the seitan sauce, maximizing your efficiency (steps 3, 4, 5, 7).
  • The ciabatta only needs a short bake; add it to the oven for just the last 5-6 minutes with the potatoes to warm through without drying. Fry the seitan kebab in the pan during this final oven stretch (steps 3, 6).
  • Gently fold the spinach into the salad right before serving to maintain its fresh texture. Crucially, set aside some spinach leaves to layer directly into your ciabatta rolls for an extra pop of green (steps 5, 8).
